Hi! I’m Roxane Dumont, a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Unit of Population Epidemiology (UEP) at the Geneva University Hospitals (HUG).

I am a researcher in population epidemiology with a background in statistics. My work focuses on social inequalities, health behaviors and adolescent well-being, with the objective of improving public health policies through scientific evidence. I am currently working on addictive and potentially addictive behaviors using data from the Specchio population-based cohort and the Bus Santé cohort.

I am interested in how the places we live, the work we do and the habits we build shape our health over time. My work sits at the intersection of population cohorts and behavioral health: I want to understand not only who is at risk, but where, when and why, so that prevention can reach the people who need it most.

Work and research

My research draws on two large population-based studies in Geneva: Bus Santé, a long-running cohort of Geneva adults, and Specchio-COVID19, a digital population cohort launched during the pandemic. Using these data, I study:

  • Addictive behaviors and dopaminergic stimulation: alcohol, substance use and screen-based behaviors, and their links with personality traits, anxiety and physical and psychological well-being.
  • Sedentary behavior and health: how sitting time is distributed across Geneva in space and time, how it relates to green and blue environments, and how it is associated with all-cause mortality.
  • Work and mental health: how workplace discrimination relates to anxiety and well-being, and through which pathways.
  • Diet: patterns of highly processed food consumption and their associations with health.
  • Women’s health: oral contraceptive use, side effects and discontinuation.

During my PhD, I published on post-COVID syndrome in children and adolescents (Nature Communications, 2022; BMJ Open, 2022) and on suicidal ideation in adolescents during the pandemic (Swiss Medical Weekly, 2024).

Background

  • Postdoctoral researcher, Geneva University Hospitals (2024 to present), with Prof. Idris Guessous and Dr. Mayssam Nehme.
  • PhD in Epidemiology, University of Geneva (2021 to 2024), with Prof. Idris Guessous and Prof. Silvia Stringhini. Thesis: Being an adolescent in times of pandemic: Concerns, challenges and opportunities, based on the SEROCoV-KIDS study, a population-based prospective cohort of more than 2,200 participants aged 6 months to 17 years. This work received the PSLS Annual Thesis Prize 2025 from the Faculty of Medicine.
  • Master in Statistics, University of Geneva, GSEM (2018 to 2020). Major in Extreme Value Theory and Robustness; thesis on hazard maps for strong earthquakes in Switzerland.
  • Bachelor in Management, University of Lausanne, HEC (2014 to 2018), including an exchange year at National Chengchi University, Taiwan.

My research has been presented at the Swiss Pediatric Society annual conference and the World Congress on Public Health, and featured in over 20 local media outlets (including RTS, Le Temps and Radio Lac).

Languages: French (native), English (C1), Spanish (B2), German (B2), Persian (B1).
